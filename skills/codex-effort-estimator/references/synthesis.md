# Parent Synthesis

Read this after the tier-selected passes complete. Preserve each pass result and its status; do not average incompatible outputs into false certainty.

## Reconcile method results

1. Keep sizing evidence, total-estimate methods, feasibility checks, risk scenarios, and coverage reviews distinct.
2. Compare differences by scope, count provenance, productivity coefficient, lifecycle inclusion, constraint, and risk assumption.
3. Group plausible total methods that share a dominant count, coefficient, lifecycle, or risk assumption. A group has one effective vote. Use the cluster procedure in [methods.md](methods.md) and `scripts/synthesize_method_clusters.py` to retain the neutral center and document any evidence-based override.
4. Apply only a demonstrably non-overlapping `missing/thin` review finding as an additive adjustment. Treat covered findings as validation and uncertain covered findings as range or scenario drivers.
5. Keep discovery effort separate from implementation effort, and AI-assisted output separate from its raw human baseline.

## Required result

Report the selected tier and reason; pass coverage with reasons; recommended range and planning center; confidence; method agreement and disagreement; assumptions, exclusions, risks, and open questions. For each total method, retain enough evidence to audit its input counts, coefficients, or three-point values.

When three-point data exists, report the variance aggregation and label WBS-derived aggregation as non-independent. Do not use fully correlated endpoint sums as the default probabilistic range.
